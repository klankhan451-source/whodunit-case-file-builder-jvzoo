Do the mysteries actually make sense?

Yes — that is the whole point of the Logic Validator. No. Whodunit Case-File Builder is a generator, not a template pack: it runs in your web browser on phone, tablet or computer, lays out every page for you, and adds the designed front and back covers automatically. Nothing to install.
